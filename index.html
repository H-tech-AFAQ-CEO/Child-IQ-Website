About Us

Passionate About
Communication & Connection

I'm Sarah Mitchell, a specialist Speech and Language Therapist with over 12 years' experience supporting children with communication and swallowing difficulties. I believe every child has a story to tell — and I'm here to help them tell it.

  • Fully qualified SLT, registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)
  • Member of the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists (RCSLT)
  • Specialist in children's speech, language, communication & feeding difficulties
  • Warm, family-centred approach with evidence-based practice at the core
  • Sessions available in-clinic, at your home, school, or via secure video call

Yes! We offer secure video sessions via a GDPR-compliant platform. Online therapy is highly effective for most children and allows us to work with families all over the UK. It's also a brilliant option if your child finds in-person therapy tricky at first.
Yes — you can ask your GP or health visitor for an NHS referral. However, NHS waiting times can be significant. Many families choose private therapy for faster access to assessment and therapy, while waiting for NHS services or in addition to them.
